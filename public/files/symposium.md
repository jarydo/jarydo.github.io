I co-lead Socratica's 2026 Symposium, which was simultaneously one of the most difficult but rewarding experiences of my life. In the end, we sold out with over 2000 attendees coming from 135 cities across the world, had over 2 million impressions on social media, raised over 6 figures with support from Anthropic, Figma, Shopify, and more, and were featured on [CBC](https://www.cbc.ca/listen/live-radio/1-104-the-morning-edition-k-w/clip/16204314-uw-students-show-passion-projects-socratica), [The Record](https://www.therecord.com/news/waterloo-region/socratica-future-of-tech-kitchener/article_728c754f-f36c-544e-be60-efc67ba4c78a.html?curius=5822), and [Be Giant](https://www.begiant.ca/stories/ideas/socratica-symposium-waterloo-university?curius=5822).

Some notable additions that had never been done before:
* An in-house engineered NFC system where attendees tap booths they find interesting, never having to take out their phones
* A 3D "Ocean" of all attendees, sharing their [hopes and dreams](https://symposium.socratica.info/ocean/) that get added as they register
* A communal ending jam session

Projects included a [computer played as a trumpet](https://x.com/KrishRShah/status/2037425807287517466), [2000 phones synced to music](https://x.com/freemanjiangg/status/2036354717903651276), and much more.
