I spent 9 months helping build the world's greatest celebration of making. No pressure.

Socratica Symposium 2026 sold out a 2,500-seat theatre, turned a historic tannery into a gallery of 70+ passion projects and 65+ live demos, and put another ~1,300 people on the livestream. One writer called it "the great Canadian demo day" — we'll take it.
