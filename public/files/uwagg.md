Free Wi-Fi is everywhere, but it always costs you something. UWAGG is a fake gallery Wi-Fi network that shows you exactly what.

Connect to "UWAGG_Guest" and a familiar captive portal pops up: name, email, "agree" to the terms. The moment you accept, your name goes up on a gallery display - right next to a live feed of the DNS queries your phone is quietly firing off in the background. Some services (LinkedIn and naughty sites) are in bright red to attract extra attention. The only way to get your name off the screen is to convince someone else to connect.

A self-contained network-art installation running on a single Raspberry Pi (hostapd, dnsmasq, Node.js), on display at the CS/FINE 383 pop-up gallery.
