Free Wi-Fi is everywhere — and it always costs you something. UWAGG is a fake gallery Wi-Fi network that shows you exactly what.

Connect to "UWAGG_Guest" and a familiar captive portal pops up: name, email, agree to the terms (pre-checked, buried in an accordion, thick with dark patterns). The moment you accept, your name goes up on a gallery display — right next to a live feed of the DNS queries your phone is quietly firing off in the background: every app and service it pings without you ever knowing. The only way to get your name off the screen is to convince someone else to connect.

A self-contained network-art installation running on a single Raspberry Pi (hostapd, dnsmasq, Node.js), shown at the CS/FINE 383 pop-up gallery. Inspired by Aram Bartholl's OFFLINE ART.
