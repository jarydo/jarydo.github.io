How well do you actually know your own face? This one messes with it — every time you blink.

You stand in front of a webcam, and each time you close your eyes, a WebGL shader subtly distorts your reflection: a hue shift here, a fish-eye bend there, your features quietly drifting apart. You can never catch it happening — your eyes are shut (that's change blindness) — but everyone else in the room can. Close them long enough and you're met with: "Do you recognize yourself?" Built in p5.js with ml5 face tracking.
