Can we ever objectively know who we are? Or is every semblance of our physical identity distorted through a third party?

Using your webcam, this piece changes your features every time you blinks. A WebGL shader subtly distorts your reflection: a hue shift, a fish-eye bend, your features quietly drifting apart. You can never catch it happening since your eyes are shut - change blindness. Close them long enough and soon you may no longer be able to recognize yourself. Built in p5.js with ml5 face tracking.

Demonstration [here](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3NVm4VLOjKY&feature=youtu.be)
