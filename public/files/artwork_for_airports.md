A generative piece for the most stressful place on earth: the airport.

Inspired by Brian Eno's Music for Airports, it's a grid of old-school split-flap displays that slowly, endlessly spell out the names of cities you could be flying to. Each column flips on its own prime-numbered rhythm, so the flaps almost never sync up — the patterns (and the little pentatonic tones that play alongside them) never quite repeat. Built in p5.js, meant to be, in Eno's words, "as ignorable as it is interesting."
