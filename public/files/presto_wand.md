The transit card for Toronto is called a PRESTO card, so I thought it would be funny if I turned it into a magic wand. Turns out, people agreed!

I dissolved it in acetone, fished out the antenna and chip, and rehoused them inside a cheap wand I bought off Amazon. Now I can tap onto the TTC like a magician (and even got cleared by a [fare inspector](https://x.com/jaryddiamond/status/1969947168359284844?s=20)).
